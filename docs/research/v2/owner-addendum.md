# Owner addendum (2026-09-06, late evening)

Product owner feedback received while the v2 design workflow was running. It binds the spec.

## De-rot must not be boring

"The de-rot section is just super rotting. It's so boring." Two lanes are required:

1. **Coding de-rot**: the existing drills (predict the output, spot the bug, trace, speed type) reworked to feel like a game: streaks, timers that build tension, combo counters, sound, a scoreboard against yourself.
2. **Pure play**: real games that have nothing to do with computing, "a place for you to be you". Examples the owner named: hold focus on a dot that moves on the screen. Design a small set of short, juicy, non-coding games (about 60 to 120 seconds each) that train attention and give a break: follow the moving dot, n-back with colours or sounds instead of code tokens, a reaction-time tap, a breathing pacer, a rhythm tap, a memory grid. They score, they count for the de-rot streak, they are allowed to be beautiful and playful, and they must run entirely in the browser with no data beyond a DrillResult.

Both lanes live under the same De-rot section with a clear switch, and the buddy's "derot" suggestions can point at either.

## Persona reminder

The product is "the program with a b": it should act like a bro, not a teacher. That applies to de-rot copy and game names too.
